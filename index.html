Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Are all your products certified Green Crackers?

Yes. All fireworks sold by Vel Agencies adhere to CSIR-NEERI green cracker guidelines with certified QR codes, 30% reduced emissions, and no banned chemical substances.

Do you offer wholesale and bulk discounts?

Yes! We provide direct factory wholesale discounts of 50% to 70% off standard festive retail prices for family orders, corporate gifts, and bulk community purchases.

Can I customize my Diwali gift box?

Absolutely. You can select your favorite items from our catalog or choose from our pre-packed Economy, Standard, and Mega Family Gift Hampers.

What are your store and ordering hours?

Monday – Saturday: 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM (open all 7 days during festival season). We are located in Tamil Nadu.

How do I place an order or check prices?

Click "Add to Cart" on any cracker in our catalog, select your quantities, and tap "Send Order via WhatsApp" to instantly submit your order list. You can also message our WhatsApp helpline directly.

What safety guidelines should we follow?

Always burst crackers in an open outdoor space away from flammable items, maintain safe distance, keep a bucket of water nearby, and ensure children are accompanied by an adult.
